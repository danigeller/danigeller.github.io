# Through My Lens

### A Personal Journey Through Science and Beyond

Welcome to my personal website!

I'm Daniel Geller, a PhD student at the Weizmann Institute of Science.

I developed this website as part of the Programming with AI course,
combining my interest in science with a creative approach to web design.

Here, I share a glimpse into my academic journey, my research on
light-sensitive proteins, and the adventures that inspire me outside
the laboratory.
